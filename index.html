Hi.

I'm Peter Nilsson.

I'm an educator, author, and musician.

I also consult and speak on AI and schools.

Here is some of my work:

Irreplaceable: How AI Changes Everything (and Nothing) in Teaching and Learning

What changes and what doesn't change in schools in an AI-saturated world? This is a research-based reflection and playbook for teachers, school leaders, and those who want to support teachers and school leaders.

Order at Amazon or Solution Tree.

Schooled: The Musical (working title)

Hamilton meets The Sound of Music meets Good Night, and Good Luck, set in and around a school. This show is currently being developed and has had staged readings at the New York Times Center and SXSW EDU.

schooledthemusical.com

The Educator's Notebook

The Educator's Notebook is a weekly newsletter that curates learning- and AI-related news from around the web for the purpose of promoting innovation in education.

educatorsnotebook.com

Athena Lab

Athena is an online platform for teachers to share practices. It's a place where teachers can find, create, and collaborate on the questions, activities, and assignments we use every day.

athenalab.io

Athena principles and research can be found at athenaprospectus.org.

Distant Reading

Distant Reading is a high school digital humanities course. Team taught and cross-listed in English and Computer Science, it earned a 2017 Wolfram Innovator Award from Wolfram Research.

distantreading.org

Sense and Sensation

Sense and Sensation is where I (infrequently) write about education, artificial intelligence, creativity, and cognitive science.

senseandsensation.com

Planning Process Illustrated

Planning Process Illustrated is an analysis of the planning process, as illustrated by teachers from around the United States.

planningprocessillustrated.com

Exercises for the Recovery of Childhood

Exercises for the Recovery of Childhood is a collection of prose poems composed by Andy Stallings, typeface designed by David Jonathan Ross, and designed for web by James Lindeman. I hope they bring you joy!

exercisesfortherecoveryofchildhood.com
