Irreplaceable: How AI Changes Everything (and Nothing) in Teaching and Learning

What changes and what doesn't change in schools in an AI-saturated world?
This is a research-based reflection and playbook for teachers, school leaders,
and those who want to support teachers and school leaders.

Order at Amazon or Solution Tree.

schooledthemusical.com

The Educator's Notebook

The Educator's Notebook is a weekly newsletter
that curates learning- and AI-related news from around the web
for the purpose of promoting innovation in education.

www.educatorsnotebook.com

Athena Lab

www.athenalab.io

Distant Reading

www.distantreading.org

Sense and Sensation
