Irreplaceable

How AI Changes Everything (and Nothing) in Teaching and Learning

Maya Bialik and Peter Nilsson · Solution Tree, 2026

Artificial intelligence is changing education, but what works in teaching and learning remains irreplaceable. Moving beyond the fear and hype, the book offers a balanced guide for a new era, with practical examples of how to use AI for research, lesson planning, and feedback in ways that enhance learning and foster creativity.
